Disclosure & Data Sources Volatility Desk surfaces options strategies, price targets, market outlook, and technical signals generated from real-time market data via the Finnhub API, supplemented with 200-day historical data from Stooq (with public CORS-proxy fallback) for moving average, RSI, IV Rank, and multi-timeframe chart calculations. Probability of Profit (POP), Greeks (Delta/Theta/Vega), and the P&L diagram on strategy cards are computed from Black-Scholes assumptions (zero rates and dividends) using estimated IV from realized volatility — not broker-quoted IV per contract. They are statistical heuristics, not predictions. Earnings dates come from Finnhub's free calendar endpoint and are used to flag IV-elevation windows. Implied Volatility (IV) shown is estimated from realized vol; true per-contract IV requires Finnhub's paid options tier or another provider. Full Option Chain requires Finnhub's paid tier. Watchlist and Journal entries are stored locally on this device only — they never leave your browser. The journal does not connect to your broker; it tracks what you manually log. Options trading involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every investor. Nothing on this page is investment advice. Verify all data with your broker before trading.
